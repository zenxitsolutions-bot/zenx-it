## Decisions made

- **Re-point tokens, don't rename them** — the file already documents this trade-off from the
  green→blue re-skin. Renaming ~570 utility usages is a large diff with a real chance of missing
  one and shipping a two-palette screen; re-pointing is one file and cannot miss. Rules out
  `text-coral` ever reading as its name, which the comment block calls out.
- **Every tint/ink pair was measured with the WCAG formula, not picked by eye.** Two failed and
  were changed: the pink `#db7e9c` on its own tint is 2.46:1, so a `--color-measure-ink` at 4.80:1
  now carries anything actually drawn in pink; and `--color-negative` went `#c0483f` → `#a83a32`
  because the delta pill's 12px bold text on `tint-red` was 4.29:1 — under AA, and only visible by
  computing it. Full table in the session output; the worst surviving pair is 4.63:1.
- **Light sidebar over the dark rail** — the brief's §4 asks for it explicitly, and with the page
  now mint rather than blue-grey, a near-black rail was the only heavy element on the screen.
- **Bottom tab bar derived from `NAV_BY_ROLE`, capped at five, drawer kept.** The brief asked for
  Home/Diet/Progress/Calls/Profile; those are client-role labels, and the app has no Profile route
  (account lives in the header dropdown). Slicing the existing per-role list gives the right five
  for a client without inventing a route, and keeps the bar honest for dietitian/admin too.
- **`card-hover` is opt-in, not applied to every surface** — a card that does nothing when clicked
  should not promise an interaction by lifting.

  of every untracked file, into the scratchpad. There is no commit or stash covering any of this
